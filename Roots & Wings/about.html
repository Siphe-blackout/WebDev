Founded on one belief: every child can become a strong reader.

We're a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Columbus, Ohio, working to close the childhood literacy gap for K–5 students through tutoring, books, and community.

Mission

To ensure every child has the foundational reading skills and access to books needed to thrive academically and beyond.

Vision

To become a leading regional model for accessible, high-impact childhood literacy support that measurably improves reading outcomes in underserved communities.

How Roots & Wings grew

2016 — A single after-school reading table

Roots & Wings began as a volunteer-run reading table at one Columbus elementary school, serving 30 students a week.

2019 — Book mailings launch

We introduced monthly book mailings to keep reading materials in students' hands between sessions, reaching families across five ZIP codes.

2022 — Summer camps and 501(c)(3) status

Roots & Wings became an independent nonprofit and launched its first summer reading camp to combat learning loss.

2026 — 1,850 students a year

Today we operate with an annual budget of roughly $1.2 million, funded by individual donors and corporate sponsors, serving students across Central Ohio.

Who we serve

Our work is built around several groups who each play a part in a child's reading journey.

K–5 Students & Families

Children in Columbus-area schools who need extra reading support, plus the families who enroll them.

Volunteers & Tutors

Community members who commit a few hours a week to one-on-one and small-group tutoring.

Schools & Educators

Local elementary schools that partner with us to identify students who would benefit most from support.

Donors & Sponsors

Individuals and corporate partners whose funding keeps tutoring, book mailings, and camps free for families.

Our Staff Team

A small, dedicated staff of program coordinators, literacy specialists, and volunteer managers.

The Wider Community

Neighbors, local businesses, and civic groups who champion literacy across Central Ohio.
