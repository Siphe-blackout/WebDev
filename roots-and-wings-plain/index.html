Every child deserves the words to find their way.

Roots & Wings gives K–5 students free tutoring, books mailed straight to their door, and summer reading camps that keep the gap from growing. Reading is the root; confidence is the wing.

Our Impact

  • 1,850+ students served each year
  • 42,000+ books mailed to date
  • 310 active volunteer tutors
  • 88% of students show reading gains within one school year

Three programs, one goal: confident readers.

Every program is designed around a simple idea — kids read better when books are close by and support is consistent.

Free Tutoring

One-on-one and small-group tutoring twice a week, in person and online, matched to each child's reading level.

See how it works →

Book Mailings

Age-matched books mailed monthly to enrolled families, building a home library one envelope at a time.

See how it works →

Summer Reading Camps

Four weeks of hands-on literacy camp that stops the "summer slide" before it starts.
